# AI-Powered Environmental Intelligence Platform

## 📌 Overview

Led product strategy and cross-functional execution for an AI-powered environmental intelligence platform designed to help organizations identify, monitor, and reduce waste leakage across waterways and surrounding ecosystems.

The initiative combined computer vision, geospatial analytics, IoT signals, field operations, and AI-driven insights to transform fragmented environmental data into actionable intelligence.

The platform demonstrated how data, AI, and digital experiences can be leveraged to address large-scale sustainability challenges while creating measurable operational and community impact.

---

## 🎯 Business Challenge

Environmental monitoring programs faced several challenges:

- Limited visibility into waste accumulation patterns
- Manual inspection and reporting processes
- Fragmented environmental data sources
- Reactive cleanup operations
- Difficulty measuring intervention effectiveness
- Lack of real-time decision support

These constraints limited scale, operational efficiency, and long-term sustainability outcomes.

## 🚀 Product Strategy

Defined a multi-year product roadmap focused on:

### Phase 1: Data Foundation

- Consolidate imagery, sensor, and field-operation datasets
- Establish environmental data governance standards
- Create unified operational dashboards

### Phase 2: AI-Powered Insights

- Deploy computer vision models
- Build waste detection and classification capabilities
- Enable geospatial hotspot identification

### Phase 3: Operational Intelligence

- Introduce predictive analytics
- Prioritize remediation efforts
- Automate field-work recommendations

### Phase 4: Ecosystem Expansion

- Scale to additional locations
- Enable self-service analytics
- Expand stakeholder reporting experiences

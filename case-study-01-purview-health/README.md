## Business Problem

As Microsoft Purview adoption grew, product and engineering teams needed a more consistent way to identify, prioritize, and address customer-impacting issues.

### Key Challenges

- High volume of customer escalations
- Limited visibility into emerging product-health trends
- Fragmented signals across telemetry, support, and customer-feedback systems
- Slow identification of recurring or systemic issues
- Reactive operational reviews
- Inconsistent prioritization of customer-impacting investments

These challenges increased support effort, delayed issue resolution, and made it harder to connect customer pain points with engineering priorities.

## Discovery and Insights

I used a combination of quantitative analysis and qualitative discovery to understand the causes of recurring customer pain points.

### Discovery Activities

- Customer-feedback analysis
- Escalation and support-trend analysis
- Product-telemetry review
- Incident-pattern assessment
- Stakeholder interviews
- Operational-process reviews
- Cross-functional working sessions

### Key Insights

1. **Customer issues were often systemic**

   Individual escalations frequently represented broader product or operational patterns rather than isolated events.

2. **Signals were fragmented**

   Product telemetry, customer feedback, support cases, and engineering data were reviewed independently, limiting end-to-end visibility.

3. **Issue detection was largely reactive**

   Some problems became visible only after customers raised support requests or escalations.

4. **Prioritization needed stronger evidence**

   Teams needed a shared framework for comparing customer impact, operational risk, issue frequency, and engineering effort.

5. **Leadership needed a consistent view**

   Executive and engineering stakeholders required a concise view of product health, risks, trends, ownership, and recommended actions.

## Product Strategy

The strategy centered on creating a repeatable, data-driven operating model for managing product health.

### Strategic Objectives

1. Improve visibility into customer and product health
2. Identify emerging issues earlier
3. Reduce recurring customer pain points
4. Connect customer signals with engineering prioritization
5. Improve operational and executive decision-making
6. Establish measurable ownership and accountability
7. Create a framework that could scale across product areas

### Product Principles

- **Customer signals should influence roadmap decisions**
- **Telemetry should enable earlier intervention**
- **Metrics should drive ownership and accountability**
- **Operational reviews should result in decisions and actions**
- **Health reporting should remain simple, consistent, and actionable**

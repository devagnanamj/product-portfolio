**Product Case Study - Purview Product Health Transformation**

**Overview: **
Led cross-functional initiatives focused on improving product health, reducing customer escalations, and creating actionable insights for engineering and support teams.

**Problem:** The product faced recurring customer pain points.

**Key challenges included:**
* High support volume
* Slow issue identification
* Reactive operations
* Limited visibility into trends
* Customer Impact

**Customers experienced:**
* Delayed issue resolution
* Increased support effort
* Reduced confidence in platform reliability
* Discovery Process

**Activities included:**
* Customer feedback analysis
* Escalation trend analysis
* Product telemetry review
* Stakeholder interviews
* Product Strategy

**Primary objectives:**
* Improve product health
* Reduce escalations
* Improve operational visibility
* Create data-driven decision making
* Execution

**Built:**
* Product health scorecards
* Executive dashboards
* Operational review mechanisms
* Health monitoring frameworks

**Collaborated with:**
* Engineering
* Support
* Customer Success

**Program Management**
* Results
* Outcomes
* Improved product health visibility
* Faster identification of issues
* Better prioritization decisions
* Reduced customer escalations
* Lessons Learned

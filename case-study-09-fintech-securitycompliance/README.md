# Financial Data Protection & AI Governance Transformation

> Data Security | Zero Trust | AI Governance | Identity & Access Management | Compliance

---

## Executive Summary

Financial institutions operate in one of the most regulated environments, managing highly sensitive customer, payment, financial, and personally identifiable information.

As adoption of cloud services, AI-powered experiences, mobile applications, and collaboration platforms increased, the organization needed stronger controls to protect sensitive data while enabling productivity and innovation.

The initiative focused on modernizing identity, security, compliance, and data-governance capabilities through a unified strategy spanning Microsoft Entra, Microsoft Purview, Zero Trust architecture, application modernization, mobile experiences, and AI governance.

The result was a secure-by-design operating model that improved visibility into sensitive data, reduced oversharing risks, strengthened compliance posture, and enabled more responsible adoption of AI-powered experiences.

## Business Challenge

The organization managed large volumes of sensitive information across collaboration platforms, email systems, business applications, mobile devices, and AI-enabled experiences.

Several challenges emerged:

- Sensitive financial data stored across multiple locations
- Increasing risk of oversharing information
- Customer and employee personally identifiable information (PII) requiring protection
- Regulatory and compliance obligations
- Limited visibility into sensitive data movement
- Legacy identity and access models
- Growing adoption of AI-powered productivity tools
- Mobile workforce requirements
- Line-of-Business applications using inconsistent security patterns

The organization required a scalable approach to protect data without negatively impacting user productivity.

Implement identity-centric security controls across people, devices, applications, and data.
